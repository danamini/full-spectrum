# Credits: 3D models

Generated by `tools/fetch_models.mjs`, which reads each model's title, author and licence from its source
(the Sketchfab API, or the Poly Pizza page) and refuses licences that forbid commercial use or changes. The
files are in `assets/models/`. The app names the model, its author and licence under the picture whenever
one is shown.

The other things in the wave view (the planets and Moon, Everest, the football pitch, the coin, the grain of
sand, the hair, the blood cell, the bacterium, the viruses, the ribosome, haemoglobin, DNA, the water molecule,
the hydrogen atom, the uranium nucleus and the proton) are modelled in code in `js/models3d.js`, with their
textures drawn in code: they are this project's own.

- **person**: [Man](https://poly.pizza/m/HMnuH5geEG) by [Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/u/Quaternius), from Poly Pizza. Licence: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Changes: textures resized to at most 1024 px and saved as WebP; compressed. Fetched 2026-10-21.
